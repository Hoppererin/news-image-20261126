## Lead

1. **US unveils new tariffs on 14 partners** — The Trump administration
   announced a new round of Section 301 tariffs on 14 major trading
   partners on Wednesday 25 November, the day before Thanksgiving:
   China up to 20%, potentially rising to 40%; the EU up to 20%;
   Vietnam, India, Taiwan, South Korea, Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand,
   Cambodia up to 25%; Japan also named. USTR Jamieson Greer announced
   them; the S&P 500 slipped on the day. Sources: Reuters, Washington
   Post, Inc., LinkedIn News.

## The day's big stories

2. **More tankers hit mines in Hormuz** — The IRGC Navy said two oil
   tankers exploded early on Wednesday after hitting naval mines in the
   Strait of Hormuz, on routes Iran calls unauthorised; later an LNG
   carrier was reported hit the same way. 37 ships were attacked in
   October, the highest monthly total since the war began (Washington
   Institute data via Reuters). Sources: Reuters, IRNA via OilPrice,
   The Freedom Press (IANS/Xinhua).
3. **Fuel tanker fire on Cairo ring road kills at least 30** — A fuel
   tanker crashed and caught fire on Cairo's Ring Road in Warraq, Giza,
   on Wednesday evening; at least 30 people killed and 25 injured,
   dozens of cars and motorcycles charred; the toll was described as
   preliminary. Sources: AP, The Guardian, Egypt Health Ministry.
4. **Ukraine hits Russian air base, knocks out five jets** — Ukraine's
   General Staff said strikes on Savasleyka air base (Nizhny Novgorod
   region) hit three MiG-31 jets and two Su-34 fighter-bombers. The US
   State Department approved a possible $622m sale of F-16 equipment
   to Ukraine the same day. Sources: Reuters, NY Post, The Independent.
5. **Israel kills second Hamas security chief in 24 hours** — An
   Israeli airstrike early on Wednesday hit an apartment in the Shati
   (Beach) refugee camp in Gaza City, killing Col. Mohammad Abuelouf,
   head of Hamas's internal security service in northern Gaza, his
   wife and two teenage daughters. A day earlier Israel killed Naeem
   Abu Nahel, Hamas's head of internal security for all Gaza.
   Sources: Reuters, Devdiscourse.
6. **EU unveils 22nd Russia sanctions; NATO to guard space** — The
   European Commission proposed its 22nd sanctions package on Monday
   23 November: about 60 Russian banks cut off from SWIFT, 70+
   shadow-fleet ships listed, Russian-flag athletes banned from EU
   events, crypto platforms targeted. NATO vowed to defend against
   space threats after reports Russia may launch a nuclear-armed
   satellite, possibly in December. Sources: Reuters, European
   Commission, IWPost.
7. **BepiColombo safe in Mercury orbit after glitch** — ESA confirmed
   on 23 November that BepiColombo is safe in a stable orbit around
   Mercury after orbit insertion on 21 November; a software glitch
   cut the burn short, leaving a 75-hour orbit instead of the planned
   58-hour one. Separation of the two orbiters is set for 9 December.
   Sources: ESA, Gizmodo.
